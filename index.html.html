Whether it’s designing efficient power systems or tinkering with automation projects, I’m all about blending theory with hands-on solutions. What I do

⚡ Power Systems

I work with electrical power systems, from generation to distribution. I’ve analyzed and optimized load flow, designed circuits for energy efficiency, and used tools like MATLAB and ETAP for simulations that ensure stability and reliability in grid operations.

📟 Embedded Systems

I design and program embedded systems using microcontrollers like Arduino and PIC. My experience includes building automation systems, sensor-based devices, and integrating IoT for real-world monitoring and control applications.

🛠 Circuit Design

I create, simulate, and test both analog and digital circuits. Using software like Proteus and Multisim, I can design PCBs, troubleshoot hardware issues, and develop solutions that are both functional and cost-effective.

🧠 Project Management

I collaborate with peers and mentors to plan, execute, and deliver engineering projects. I focus on efficient timelines, clear documentation, and aligning technical objectives with real-world applications—whether academic or industry-based.
